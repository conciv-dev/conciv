# Consolidated review report: `docs/designs/design-driver.md`, revision 4

Date: 2026-11-01. Stages: zoom out, comprehension, critic (design, structural, style), implementation readiness, codebase details. Seven reports in this directory. Verdict on revision 4: changes required. Rulings 1 to 20 are folded in consistently and every round 3 blocker that was a fact error is fixed. What remains is of two kinds: mechanisms the document names that do not exist or do not behave as stated (the server-to-tab channel, the live rrweb mirror, the client config channel), and loop rules that produce wrong stops. Items marked **ruling needed** change the design and wait for Omri; everything else has one defensible fix and goes into revision 5.
