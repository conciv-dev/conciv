The session picker previews the highlighted session beside the list: a read-only thread of its transcript, newest messages first with earlier pages loading on scroll, the search words marked, `Alt+ArrowRight` to read it and `Alt+ArrowLeft` back to the search, Escape closing the preview before the picker. Core serves it through the read-only `sessions.preview` procedure. Search marks now come from Ark `Highlight` and zag `highlightWord`, and keyboard hints share one ui-kit-system `Kbd`. `Thread.Messages` takes `readOnly`, which leaves the assistant action bar with Copy only; the preview sets it. With All projects on, another project's hit previews too: `sessions.preview` takes that hit's `cwd`, and the preview header copies its resume command through the same `createResumeCopy` as the row, so both show Copied together. `ClipboardCopyButton` can reflect a shared `ClipboardCopy` instead of its own. Previewing a session from a folder the search index does not cover yet adds that folder to the index in the background. The preview opens after 600 ms, closes when its session leaves the results, and its header copy button keeps its own Copied state with a 44px hit area.
