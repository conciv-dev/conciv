Typing `@` in the composer also lists up to five other sessions, after the skills and agents, as opencode's composer does: this project's sessions on a bare `@`, other projects' once a title word is typed. A session row shows its title with a session icon, and picking one inserts `@<title>`. A `ui-kit-tap` trigger item takes an optional `insert`, the text its picked chip shows and lowers to.
