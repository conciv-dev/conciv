### 5. Odd things / rule breaks

- Copied test (opencode). Keep its names; the house "no `Of` suffix", "no abbreviations" rules don't apply to it.
- No mocks, spies, fake timers or sleeps. Concurrency is driven with `Deferred` gates: good.
- Scheduling assumption: `Effect.yieldNow` before publish (128, 144, 219, 331, 587, 608, 683, 704, 1323) assumes one yield is enough for the subscriber fiber to attach. 563 asserts `observed` after one `yieldNow` to prove nothing interleaved: a negative assertion after a single yield can pass vacuously.
- Tests reach into storage: rows of `EventTable`/`EventSequenceTable` and probe tables created by raw SQL (262, 286, 524, 1002-1252). 524 interpolates event text into SQL.
- Product test hooks exist only for these tests: `beforeAggregateRead` and `logReadPageSize` in `Bus.configured` (639, 1336, 1364). Under the house rule "no product code for tests" they would be refused if written here; they come with the opencode copy.
- 857 asserts only `created === 0` while its title claims a decode of transformed values.
- Two definitions share `test.versioned` v2 with different schemas (`VersionedMessageV2` at 73 and `VersionedMessage` at 92).
- Non-null assertion at 978 (`published.durable!.seq`).
