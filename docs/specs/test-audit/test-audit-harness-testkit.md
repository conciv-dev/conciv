### 5. Odd things / rule breaks

- No `vi.fn`/`vi.mock`/`vi.spyOn`. `vi` is imported for `vi.waitFor` only (692).
- Timing guesses: fixed sleeps of 20-50 ms before asserting absence (565, 826, 843, 903, 926); wall-clock span asserts against `PACE_MS × steps × PACE_TOLERANCE` (843, 860); `performance.now()` < 500 ms (955). Absence after a fixed sleep passes vacuously on a slow machine; the span asserts can flake on a loaded one. The kit should take a clock (fake timers are allowed per house rules).
- Exports with no consumer kept alive by their tests: `harnessModes`, `until({failWhen})`, `ScriptedRun.hold()`.
- 254 spawns a child process that busy-writes for 150 ms: bounded, but it is CPU while the suite runs in parallel.
- 1013 sets a V8 flag process-wide and forces two GCs.
