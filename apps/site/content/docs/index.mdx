---
title: What is conciv
description: An AI dev agent inside your running app. It sees the page you are on and changes it for you.
icon: PixelPage
---

conciv puts an AI dev agent inside the app you are already running. It reads the page you are on, drives it, edits
your source and runs your tests. You stay in the app the whole time.

## Install

Copy this prompt into your coding agent, such as Claude Code or Codex. Pick your framework first.

<SetupPrompt />

The agent runs `conciv init`, starts your dev server and logs you in. A conciv button then shows in the corner of
your app.

Then ask your agent, in your terminal or in the conciv panel:

<CopyPrompt text="Look at the page I have open in conciv. Change the main heading to say Hello from conciv. Edit the source file, then check that the page shows the new text." />

<Callout>
  conciv runs in dev builds only. The chat needs an agent CLI on your `PATH`, such as `claude`, and logged in.
</Callout>
