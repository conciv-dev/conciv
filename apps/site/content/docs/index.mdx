---
title: What is conciv
description: An AI dev agent inside your running app. Chat, page control and your dev server, added to your dev build from your bundler config.
---

conciv puts an AI dev agent inside the app you are already running. Add conciv to your Vite config, and a conciv
button appears in your dev build. Open it to chat with an agent that sees the page you are on, drives and edits it,
and reads your dev server, without leaving the app. The name is the pitch: say `@conciv/it` out loud, and conceive
it.

```ts title="vite.config.ts"
import {defineConfig} from 'vite'
import conciv from '@conciv/it/plugin/vite'

export default defineConfig({
  plugins: [conciv()],
})
```
