conciv has three running parts. Your dev server starts the engine, and adds the widget to your page. The widget talks
to the engine over one websocket. The engine runs the agent CLI behind the chat.

## The parts

- **Build plugin.** `@conciv/it` in your Vite, webpack, Rspack or Next.js config. In development it starts the engine
  and adds the widget.
- **Engine.** Runs in node, in your dev server's process. It listens on `127.0.0.1` only, on its own port.
- **Widget.** The conciv button, the panel and the terminal. It mounts in a shadow root, so your styles and its styles
  stay apart.
- **Harness.** The agent CLI the engine runs a turn with, such as Claude Code. See [Harnesses](/docs/harnesses).

The engine answers only browsers that logged in. See [Logging in](/docs/usage/logging-in).

## A turn

<Steps>
  <Step>You send a message. The widget sends it to the engine over the websocket.</Step>
  <Step>The engine runs the turn with your harness, in your project folder, with conciv's tools.</Step>
  <Step>The agent calls tools: it reads the page, drives it, edits files and runs tests.</Step>
  <Step>A risky call waits on a permission card in the panel. It goes on once you answer.</Step>
  <Step>Text, tool calls and cards stream back into the panel.</Step>
</Steps>

If the socket drops, the widget dials it again. A running turn resumes from its last event.

## Where state lives

The engine keeps its state in `.conciv/` at your project root. The folder has its own `.gitignore`. It ignores
everything except `settings/`.

<Files>
  <Folder name=".conciv" defaultOpen>
    <File name="server.json" />
    <File name="trusted-browsers.json" />
    <File name="conciv.db" />
    <Folder name="settings" />
    <Folder name="bin" />
  </Folder>
</Files>

- `server.json`: the engine port and token. The engine reuses the port on its next start.
- `trusted-browsers.json`: the browsers that logged in.
- `conciv.db`: sessions, chat history and saved permission answers, in SQLite.
- `settings/`: project settings saved from the widget. It is not ignored, so you can commit it.
- `bin/conciv`: the conciv command, first on the agent's `PATH`.
