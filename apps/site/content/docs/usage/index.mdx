Once conciv is in your dev build, a conciv button sits in the corner of your app.

<ImageZoom
  src="/screenshots/widget-fab.png"
  alt="The conciv robot button in the corner of a running app"
  width={1920}
  height={1080}
/>

Click it to open the chat panel. The first time, a browser asks for the code your dev server's terminal prints: see
[Logging in](/docs/usage/logging-in). From the panel you talk to the agent, watch it work, and answer what it asks.

<ImageZoom src="/screenshots/panel-open.png" alt="The conciv chat panel open over the app" width={1920} height={1080} />
