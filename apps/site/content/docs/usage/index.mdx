Start your dev server and open your app. A conciv button sits in the corner of the page.

<ImageZoom
  src="/screenshots/widget-fab.png"
  alt="The conciv robot button in the corner of a running app"
  width={1920}
  height={1080}
/>

Click it to open the chat. Then ask for the outcome you want, about the page you are looking at:

```text
Make the heading on this page larger and save it to the source.
```

The agent reads the page, changes it, and edits your source file. A risky action shows a card first, and waits for
you to allow it.

<ImageZoom src="/screenshots/panel-open.png" alt="The conciv chat panel open over the app" width={1920} height={1080} />

## Add what is not built in

These come with conciv, with nothing to add:

- the chat, sessions and the quick terminal
- logging in, approvals and settings
- page control and the element picker
- the dev server tools
- the **Terminal** tab, on a harness that has one

Live tests, the whiteboard, the recorder, the TanStack tools and the iOS simulator come from plugins. Add the ones you
want to `plugins` in your conciv config. [Plugins conciv ships](/docs/plugins/built-ins) names each one and its
package.
