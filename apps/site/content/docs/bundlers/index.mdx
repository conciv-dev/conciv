title: Rollup and esbuild
description: conciv does not run with Rollup or esbuild yet. Serve your app with Vite instead.
---

conciv does not run with Rollup or esbuild yet. Serve your app with Vite, and add conciv there. Vite builds with
Rollup, so most Rollup apps move over with few changes.

Once your app runs on Vite, copy this prompt into your coding agent:

<SetupPrompt framework="vite" />

See [Vite](/docs/bundlers/vite) for the manual steps.

## Edge cases

<Accordions>
<Accordion id="entries" title="The Rollup and esbuild plugin entries">

`@conciv/it/plugin/rollup` and `@conciv/it/plugin/esbuild` exist, but do nothing. They start no engine and add no
widget.

</Accordion>
<Accordion id="init" title="Running conciv init on a Rollup or esbuild project">

init installs `@conciv/it`. Then it prints a card that says the entry does nothing today, and points you to Vite. It
does not edit your config.

</Accordion>
</Accordions>
