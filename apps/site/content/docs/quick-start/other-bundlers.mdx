---
title: Rollup and esbuild
description: conciv does not run with Rollup or esbuild yet.
---

<Callout type="warn">
  `@conciv/it/plugin/rollup` and `@conciv/it/plugin/esbuild` exist but do nothing: they do not start the engine or add
  the widget.
</Callout>

`conciv init` on a Rollup or esbuild project installs `@conciv/it`, then prints a card that says so instead of wiring
your config.

To use conciv, serve your app with Vite and add conciv to the Vite config. Vite uses Rollup under the hood, so most
Rollup apps can move to the Vite plugin directly. See [Vite](/docs/quick-start/vite).
